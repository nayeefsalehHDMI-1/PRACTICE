About ShareBite

Empowering our neighborhood to reduce food waste and support one another.

MISSION

Reducing Food Waste

Millions of tons of perfectly good food go to waste every year. ShareBite bridges the gap between surplus food from local businesses/households and neighbors who can use it.

COMMUNITY

Stronger Together

Our platform connects local bakeries, grocery stores, and community members in real time, making sharing surplus food transparent, safe, and entirely free.

HOW IT WORKS

Simple & Fast

Visit our main app page to list surplus items with their exact location and pickup times, or browse available fresh foods nearby to claim them instantly.
